Disaster Risk Matrix

The system combines rainfall, river level, reservoir stress, soil saturation, slope, drainage, settlement exposure, historical events, road conditions, and climate trends.

Flood Risk

River overflow, drainage failure, reservoir pressure, rainfall anomaly, and low-lying settlement exposure.

MEDIUM RISK

Landslide Risk

Slope instability, soil saturation, road cutting, vegetation loss, and continuing rainfall.

MEDIUM RISK

Climate Change Signal

Long-term rainfall shift, extreme-weather frequency, temperature anomaly, and seasonal pattern deviation.

LOW SIGNAL

Route Disruption

Blocked roads, bridge exposure, alternate-route availability, travel time, and secondary hazard risk.
